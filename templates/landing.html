🍱 TiffinTrack

Fair subscription billing for home-style tiffin services.

Customers pay only for the days they actually receive their tiffin.

Owner Login Create Account
🎯 Who it's for

Home-style tiffin and meal-delivery owners who manage monthly subscriptions and need fair billing when customers pause their plans.

⚡ Key Features
  • Pause and resume subscriptions
  • Automatic pro-rated billing
  • Mid-cycle subscription transfer
  • Morning delivery notifications
  • Search, sorting and pagination
  • Messy customer-data import
💡 How it helps

Instead of manually calculating paused days and transfer periods, the system keeps subscription history and automatically calculates each customer's served-day share.

  1. SMS/WhatsApp bill and delivery reminders.
  2. Customer self-service pause/resume.
  3. Daily delivery route planning.
