🍱 TiffinTrack

Fair billing for home-style tiffin services — pay only for the days you actually eat.

Owner Login Create Account
Who it's for

Any tiffin/meal-delivery owner who bills customers monthly but needs to account for days a customer pauses their plan (travel, festivals, sick days).

Key features
  • Pause / resume with exact dates
  • Automatic pro-rated monthly bill
  • Search customers by name or phone
  • Sortable, paginated customer list
How it helps

No more manual spreadsheets or arguments about "I was out of town for a week." The bill is calculated automatically from real pause dates, down to the rupee.

What we'd build next
  1. SMS/WhatsApp reminders when a customer's bill is ready.
  2. A self-service link so customers can pause/resume their own plan.
  3. Delivery route planning for the day's active customers.
